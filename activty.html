Discover how different techniques can completely transform your daily cup of coffee.

1. Pour Over

The pour over method highlights the delicate, nuanced flavor notes of single-origin beans. By manually pouring hot water over coffee grounds in a conical filter, you have complete control over extraction time and water temperature.

Key Characteristics:

  • Flavor Profile: Clean, crisp, and bright
  • Grind Size: Medium-fine (similar to sea salt)
  • Brew Time: 3 minutes

2. French Press

The French press is an immersion brewing method that yields a heavy, rich, and full-bodied cup. Because it uses a metal mesh filter rather than paper, the coffee's natural oils pass right into your mug.

Key Characteristics:

  • Flavor Profile: Bold, robust, and textured
  • Grind Size: Coarse (similar to breadcrumbs)
  • Brew Time: 4 minutes

3. Espresso

Espresso is the foundation of many popular coffee drinks like lattes and cappuccinos. It is made by forcing pressurized hot water through finely-ground coffee beans, resulting in a concentrated shot topped with a golden crema.

Key Characteristics:

  • Flavor Profile: Intense, concentrated, and rich
  • Grind Size: Very fine (similar to powdered sugar)
  • Brew Time: 25 to 30 seconds
